Colorizing Photos of Space

Fun Facts

James Webb Telescope has 2 main cameras that capture images in infrared light:
  • Near-Infrared Camera (NIRCam)
  • Mid-infrared Instrument (MIRI)
NIRCam filters are designed to detect:
  • Hydrogen
  • Iron
  • Water
  • Methane
NIRCam uses 29 filters and MIRI uses 9 filters to isolate specific wavelengths of light for detailed study.

Color is applied chromatically.
  • Blue: shortest wavelengths
  • Green: medium wavelengths
  • Red: longest wavelengths

Webb's dynamic range is so vast, 1 pixel can be one of over 65,000 shades of gray.
